Bid without a smartphone

Around half the contractors in this state do not own a computer, and the ones who do not are the ones agents charge to bid on their behalf. The Bureau runs staffed desks where an officer sits with you and enters your bid — free, and on the same register as everyone else.

Where the desks are

{% for lga in lgas %} {% endfor %}
Staffed desks. Opening hours are the state's working hours; call ahead if you are travelling from another LGA.
Local government areaWhat they can do for youHours
{{ lga }} Register a company, attach certificates, fill and submit a bid, print a receipt. {{ SUPPORT_HOURS }}

Desk staff cannot submit a bid you have not read, cannot set your password, and cannot see the prices of other bidders. Everything they do is recorded against their own staff account on the same ledger as an MDA officer's actions.

What to bring

  • Your CAC certificate (the original, or a photograph of it).
  • Your tax identification number, and your tax clearance if you have one.
  • A phone number that reaches you, so the desk can send you the receipts.
  • Your bank account details, if you are registering for payment.

You do not need to bring a USB drive, and you will not be asked to pay anything.

If someone asks you for money

Nobody may charge a fee to register a supplier or to submit a bid. If an agent, a staff member or anyone else asks you for money to "process" a registration or to "make sure" your bid is seen, that is corruption and you can report it anonymously: the whistleblower channel takes reports without an account and without your name.
