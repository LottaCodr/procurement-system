Reverse auctions

A reverse auction is a bid opening turned into a live process: pre-qualified bidders compete on price in a fixed window. Published here as rank and distance from the leading price — never as other bidders' amounts. Competitors see the pressure they are under; nobody is handed each other's commercial terms.

What is public while the auction runs

Your own price, your rank, and how far you are from the best price. Everyone's price becomes public when the auction closes. Absolute prices of losing bids are never published, because a bid is a firm's cost structure, not a public document.
