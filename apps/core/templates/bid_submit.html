How submission works

  1. You prepare a priced bid against the criteria below, in the form the tender documents specify.
  2. You submit it sealed. The bid is encrypted in your browser before it leaves your device. The server stores a ciphertext and a commitment hash — it cannot read your price either, which is the point: a system administrator cannot sell what the system cannot see.
  3. You get a receipt immediately with the commitment hash and a verification link. That hash is published at the public opening, so a bid substituted afterwards is provable.
  4. Bids are opened publicly at {{ t.opening_at|datetime_wat }} {% if t.opening_venue %}at {{ t.opening_venue }}{% endif %}. You may attend.
  5. Scores are published against the criteria, with the committee named and every dissent recorded.
