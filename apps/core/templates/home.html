Every action on this platform — publishing a tender, opening bids, signing a contract, certifying a payment — is written to an append-only ledger, each entry chained to the one before it by SHA-256. Corrections are new entries, never edits. The head hash is published so you can check we have not rewritten anything since you last looked.
