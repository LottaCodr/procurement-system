A flag is a prompt to look, not a finding of guilt

Each trigger prints the arithmetic that produced it, so a bidder can rebut it and a journalist can reproduce it. Suspicion that cannot be checked is rumour, and rumour is not evidence.

What happens after a flag

Anyone can freeze a suspect process

A bidder, a losing competitor or any member of the public can request a freeze: a ten-day suspension of the procedure while an independent panel looks at it. The Bureau cannot refuse to record the request.

How to request a freeze or file an objection

The panel is not the Bureau's alone

Half the seats on every review panel are nominated by civil society, and the Bureau cannot reject those nominees. A panel the accused appoints is not a review.
