What a flag is not

A flag is a prompt for scrutiny, not a finding of guilt. Every flag links the evidence it was computed from so a bidder can rebut it, and a freeze — a 10-day suspension of the procedure pending an independent panel — can be requested by any bidder or any member of the public, not only by officials.
