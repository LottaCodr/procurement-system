What an objection needs to contain

  1. What you say went wrong, in your own words: the rule you believe was broken and the decision you are asking the panel to change.
  2. The facts you rely on, with dates and references where you have them. You do not need to attach documents to file, and the panel will ask for them if it needs them.
  3. Who you are, or nothing at all. An objection may be filed anonymously; the published file will say "anonymous complainant".

Filing starts a ten-day freeze. That is not a courtesy to the objector: it is the only way a review can be meaningful once the contract has been signed and paid.

What happens to what you write

The text of your objection is published on the register, because a challenge that is decided in private cannot be checked. If any part of your statement is commercially sensitive, say so in the text and the Bureau will record the fact of a redaction rather than silently dropping it.
