Objections on this process

{{ objections|length }} objection{{ objections|length|pluralize }} filed. An objection is not a complaint in a drawer: filing one freezes the procedure for ten days while a panel — half of it nominated by civil society — decides whether the award stands.

Who can object, and when

Any bidder or member of the public may object inside the published window, which is printed on the award. The window is a real limit: after it closes the freeze route closes with it. If you believe the decision was unlawful outside that window, the route is the courts, and the register stays as evidence either way.
