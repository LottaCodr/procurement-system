Standards and guarantees

  • OCDS 1.1 with a versioned taraba extension — LGA, method, approval body, red flags, immutable-from timestamp, commitment root. Extensions never fork the core schema.
  • Continuous releases. A new release is emitted when a process changes, not in a nightly dump.
  • Every release is schema-validated before it leaves the API. A transparency claim that cannot be executed is decoration; GET /api/v1/schema.json is what we validate against.
  • Permanent URLs. /tenders/{ocid}/ never changes. Retired paths 301 to successors, and the whole map is published at /redirects. Kano’s re-platforming turned every previously published award list and OCDS report into a 404; that is treated here as data destruction, with a test written against it.
  • Reproducible integrity. python manage.py verify_ledger recomputes the whole hash chain from the public bulk dump. You do not have to believe us.
