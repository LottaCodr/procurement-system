What this step needs

{% if step == 1 %}

Your company's legal identity, exactly as it appears on the CAC certificate. The legal name is published on the supplier register, so it must be the registered name rather than a trading name.

{% elif step == 2 %}

A named contact who answers. Notices go to this person by email and SMS, and it is the address the Bureau will use if it needs to reach you about a bid.

{% elif step == 3 %}

Your certificates, with expiry dates. The platform will suspend bidding if a certificate lapses, so entering the real date protects you from losing a bid to a paperwork surprise.

{% elif step == 4 %}

What you are capable of: the number of similar contracts you have delivered, your annual turnover, and the classification you are applying for.

{% elif step == 5 %}

Who owns the company. Anyone with 5% or more must be listed. If an owner is a politically exposed person, that is declared here and published on your profile — declaring it is not a disqualification, and concealing it is.

{% else %}

Check everything, then submit. You will receive a reference number and an itemised list of what the reviewer still needs, if anything.

{% endif %}
