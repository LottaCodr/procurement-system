Register to bid

Registration is free, takes about twenty minutes, and needs no agent. You will need your CAC registration number, your tax identification number, and scans or phone photographs of your certificates. The platform keeps your draft for thirty days, so you can start on a phone and finish later.

Before you start

  • Your RC number and TIN. The platform checks both against the registries and records the check and its date on your file.
  • Certificate expiry dates. A tax clearance that lapses suspends your bidding automatically — it will not surprise you at a submission desk.
  • Your beneficial owners. Anyone holding 5% or more, with their percentage. This is the register that stops a company belonging to the official who awards its contract.
  • A phone number you answer. All notices are sent by SMS as well as email, because most bidders here do not read email daily.

What happens after you submit

  1. You get a reference number immediately, and a copy of everything you submitted.
  2. A Bureau officer checks your documents against the registry records. The check, its date and the officer's role are recorded on your file.
  3. If something is missing, you are told exactly what — with the specific field named, not "application failed".
  4. Once approved you can bid on any open tender, and your profile appears in the supplier register with the same information everyone else can see.

Start or continue

You do not need an account to begin. Your draft is held against a private link, so nothing is published until you submit it.

Begin registration

If you registered before and want to see your entries, use the private link you were given when you saved. If you have lost it, telephone the Bureau and quote your company name and RC number.

Help with the form

Assisted desks in each local government area will fill the form in with you, free of charge. Find the nearest desk, or telephone {{ CONTACT_PHONE }}.

No one may charge you a fee to register. Anyone who does is committing an offence and you can report it anonymously.
