How these rules are actually enforced

At publication

A process cannot be published without a disclosed estimate, and the advertisement period is checked against the band before the notice goes live. A short-cut period is refused, not flagged.

At award

Awards must name the approving authority for their band, and awards that sit just under a boundary raise the threshold-shaving indicator automatically.

Bid security

The cap is a maximum, not a target. Anything above it is refused by the platform, because a security a small firm cannot raise is an eligibility filter dressed as a formality.

If a rule was broken

Every process page carries the rule that applied and the figures it was checked against. You can challenge it.
