How the grade is computed

  • Timeliness (35%) — milestones delivered against the planned dates.
  • Quality (40%) — snags and rework found at inspection.
  • Variation control (25%) — growth of the contract value after signature.

A: 85+ · B: 70–84 · C: 50–69 · D: 30–49 · F: below 30 or defaulted. Scores are recorded by a named officer and can be disputed.
