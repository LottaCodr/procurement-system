What happens next

  1. The report is encrypted and stored. Its reference is logged; its contents are not.
  2. The Bureau's integrity unit opens it and records a triage decision.
  3. If it relates to a live procurement, an investigator can freeze the procedure for ten days while it is examined.
  4. The outcome — dismissed, referred, or acted on — is recorded against your reference. You can check it with the reference alone.
The honest limits of this channel
  • It is not Tor-grade anonymity. The Bureau cannot read your report, but the network can see that someone visited this page.
  • If you need to remain unknown to the state itself, report through a journalist or a civil society organisation you trust, or use a public computer in another town.
  • This channel is for corruption in public procurement. Threats to life should go to the police.

What to include to make it actionable

  • The tender reference or the project, and the MDA involved.
  • What was demanded, offered or decided, and the date.
  • Which rule was broken — the threshold table and the red-flag indicators are published so you can check.
  • Anything unusual you noticed: a short advertisement window, a company that always wins, a price far above the market.
