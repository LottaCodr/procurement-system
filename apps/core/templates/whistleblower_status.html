If the status has not moved, that is what the register says. A status is not a promise of an outcome: the record shows what has actually been done, including the times when nothing has.

What the statuses mean
  • Received — the report is stored, encrypted, and waiting to be read.
  • Triaged — an investigator has read it and decided whether it can be acted on.
  • Investigating — work is under way, which may include freezing a live procurement for ten days.
  • Resolved — action was taken or the matter was referred elsewhere; any outcome summary the Bureau publishes appears against the reference.
  • Dismissed — no further action. The register records that it is dismissed; it does not tell you why, and that limit is stated rather than hidden.
