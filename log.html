New here? How to become a vendor & bid

How to bid for Taraba State contracts

  1. Register your company and pay the one-time registration fee for your category.
  2. Complete your profile and upload your CAC certificate, tax clearance and other statutory documents.
  3. Get verified by the Bureau — you're notified by email once your documents are approved.
  4. Browse tenders open to your vendor type and submit a sealed bid before the closing date.
  5. Win & deliver — awards, contracts, payments and progress are all tracked here.

Everything is done online — no trip to Jalingo. Bids are sealed and cannot be seen by anyone until the public opening ceremony. The Bureau never asks for payment through any personal account.
