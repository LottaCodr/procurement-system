Vendor Registration

Register your company to bid for contracts advertised by Taraba State ministries, departments and agencies. Registration takes about ten minutes and is valid for one year.

You will need your CAC certificate, TIN, and your tax, PenCom, ITF and NSITF compliance certificates. Your progress is saved at any point, so you can gather anything missing and return without starting again.
